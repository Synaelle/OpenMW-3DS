# Contributing

Contributions are welcome! Bug fixes, performance work, better menus on the two screens, translations of the port's
own text, anything that makes Morrowind play better on the 3DS.

A few rules:

* **Stay in the project's scope.** This is a port of OpenMW to the 3DS. Changes that don't serve that, or that belong
  in OpenMW itself, will be declined. When in doubt, open an issue and ask first.
* **No game assets.** Never include files from Morrowind or its expansions (textures, models, sounds, game data),
  even converted. The port only works from the player's own copy.
* **Test on a real console** if you can, a New 3DS, New 3DS XL or New 2DS XL. Say in your pull request how you tested.
* **One change per pull request**, with a short explanation of what it does and why.

By contributing, you agree that your work is published under the GNU General Public License v3.0, like the rest of
the project.
