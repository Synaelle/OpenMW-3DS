OpenMW 3DS
A port of the OpenMW engine to the New Nintendo 3DS.

Unofficial fan project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Bethesda Softworks, ZeniMax Media or Nintendo. It contains no game content: it needs your own legally owned copy of The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind.
